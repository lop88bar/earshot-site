A NotebookLM alternative built for listening

NotebookLM is a research notebook where audio is one feature among many. Earshot starts from the opposite end: it only makes audio, and makes it properly. Your notes, PDFs or any topic become produced episodes, with a real narrative voice, music, sound design and a series identity, in a mobile app designed around the moments you actually listen.

Where the two tools differ

Audio-first, not audio-alsoEvery Earshot episode is written for the ear: essay structure, recaps, pacing. Not a summary that happens to be spoken.
A show, not a clipSeries keep their voice, music and announcement over time. Episode 7 sounds like episode 1, which makes long material followable.
On your phoneChat to create, player with word-synced transcript, push when an episode is ready. Built for commutes, not desktops.

When NotebookLM is the right choice

Honestly: if you need to interrogate a pile of sources, ask questions with citations and get grounded answers in text, NotebookLM is excellent and Earshot does not try to replace that. Earshot is for when the output you want is listening time: an exam syllabus turned into a study series, a briefing before a call, a field you want to learn during commutes, a story to get lost in.

Frequently asked questions

How is Earshot different from NotebookLM's audio overviews?

NotebookLM is a research tool where audio is one output. Earshot only does audio: episodes written for listening, a consistent narrator, music and sound design, series identity, in a mobile app.

Can it work from my documents?

Yes: PDFs, photos of notes, links, or just a topic in chat. A whole course can become a multi-episode curriculum.

Does it generate audio in languages other than English?

Italian and English, each built natively: script, narration, pronunciation and mastering per language, not translated as an afterthought.
