Turn text into a podcast worth listening to

Earshot turns any text into a podcast episode: an article, a PDF, your notes, an email thread before a call, or a single chat message with a topic. The difference from text-to-speech converters is the word "into": your text is the source material, and what comes out is a written, narrated, scored and mastered episode.

How it works

1. Send the textPaste it, link it, upload the PDF, or photograph the page. One chat message is enough to start.
2. The producer writes the episodeA script shaped for listening: structure, pacing, an opening and a close. Then narration, music and sound design.
3. Listen anywhereThe episode lands in your library with a word-synced transcript, and stays editable: title, tone, length, structure.

What people make with it

  • Call prep: an email thread and a brief become an 8-12 minute briefing, ready before you get in the car.
  • Study: lecture notes and slides become a series that covers the syllabus, recaps included.
  • Learning a field: a topic plus optional sources becomes a multi-episode curriculum, deep not broad.
  • Stories: narrative episodes with full scene-by-scene sound design, when you want to get lost in something.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of text can I turn into a podcast?

Articles and links, PDFs, notes, an email thread plus a brief, or one message describing what you want to hear. The producer writes an episode from it rather than reading it back.

Is this the same as text-to-speech?

No. TTS converts words to a voice one for one. Earshot writes a script shaped for listening, narrates it naturally, adds music and sound design, and masters the result like a real production.

Can I control length, tone and format?

Yes: generate immediately and let the producer decide, or set length (about 5, 15 or 30 minutes), format and tone. Episodes stay editable after generation.
