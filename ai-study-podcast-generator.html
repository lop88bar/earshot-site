Turn your notes into a study podcast

Earshot is an AI study podcast generator: send a photo of your lecture notes, a PDF of the slides, or just the topic of your next exam, and it writes, narrates and mixes real episodes that cover your material. Not text-to-speech reading your notes back: a produced show, built to be listened to on the way to class.

How it works

1. Send your materialA photo of notes, a PDF, links, or just the exam topic in chat. One message is enough.
2. The producer builds a seriesEpisodes structured around your syllabus, essay-style, deep not broad, each closing with a recap of what matters.
3. Listen, review, reshapeWord-synced transcript for revision, and every episode stays editable: change the length, tone or structure.

Made for studying, not for reading aloud

Most "notes to audio" tools are converters: they take your text and read it. That is fine for a paragraph, and exhausting for a syllabus. Earshot approaches your material the way a producer would:

  • Coverage as a series: a whole course becomes a multi-episode curriculum, not one endless file.
  • Structured for memory: essay-form episodes with take-away recaps, so listening twice actually compounds.
  • A consistent show: each series keeps its voice, music and identity, which makes it feel like a program you follow rather than generated audio.
  • Transcript synced word by word: jump from what you heard to what was said when you revise.

Frequently asked questions

Can I really turn my lecture notes into a podcast?

Yes. Send a photo of handwritten notes, a PDF of the slides, or textbook chapters. The producer structures them into episodes covering your syllabus, each with a recap, narrated with natural voice, music and sound design.

How is this different from text-to-speech?

Text-to-speech reads your notes back word for word. Earshot writes an actual episode from them: restructured for listening, explained in essay form, produced with a consistent voice and score.

What can I send as study material?

PDFs, photos of notes, links, or just the topic typed in chat. Follow-up questions are optional by construction; you can always skip and let the producer decide.
